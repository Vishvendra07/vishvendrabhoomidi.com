## Four starting points

- [Workflow Desk](/work/workflow-desk) · A two-session React case queue with optimistic concurrency and recovery from a stale save.
- [Intake Evals](/work/intake-evals) · An extraction regression harness with evidence, corrections and intentional abstention.
- [Event Relay](/work/event-relay) · A transactional outbox with worker leases and repeat-safe downstream receipts.
- [Implementation Kit](/work/implementation-kit) · Two simulated CRM implementations, a shared import, and an operator rollback exercise.

## Product interfaces

- [EatFit Order Sandbox](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/eatfit-order-sandbox) · A repeat-safe checkout with stock and price snapshot tests.
- [Property Data Explorer](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/property-data-explorer) · Linked filters on a map and table, plus a DuckDB query plan.
- [Document Review Queue](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/document-review-queue) · Evidence flags and versioned human review decisions.

## Evaluation and agent systems

- [NutriLens OCR Bench](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/nutrilens-ocr-bench) · OCR and field extraction results on generated nutrition labels.
- [Voice Turn Trace](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/voice-turn-trace) · A replay of overlapping voice stages and an interrupted turn.
- [Tenant Search Benchmark](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/tenant-search-benchmark) · Scoped keyword and vector retrieval with a pgvector cross-check.
- [MCP Approval Demo](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/mcp-approval-demo) · A tool proposal and an operator-approved action tied to the exact payload.
- [Model Change Review](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/model-change-review) · Two trained classifiers, paired errors, and routing coverage.

## Backend and delivery

- [Event Relay Performance](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/event-relay-performance) · A measured local comparison of one and three workers.
- [Postgres CDC Projection](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/postgres-cdc-projection) · Checkpoint a logical stream and replay a batch after restart.
- [Durable Approvals](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/durable-approvals) · Resume a Temporal workflow after worker replacement and release work on expiry.
- [Subscription Reconciliation Lab](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/subscription-reconciliation-lab) · Rebuild a projection when a missing sequence arrives late.
- [API Contract Compatibility Gate](https://github.com/Vishvendra07/api-contract-compatibility) · Catch and repair two real consumer contract breaks in a sandbox.
